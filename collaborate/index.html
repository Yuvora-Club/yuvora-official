Collaborate & join

Let’s make something together.

One form for everything: joining as a member, partnering, co-hosting, workshops, new hubs, volunteering and sponsorship. Prefer email?

Six good ways in

  • ✳Join as a member — pick your hub(s) and show up. Beginners get the warmest welcome.
  • 1Partner with Yuvora — schools and clubs growing the community with us.
  • 2Co-host an event — competitions, meetups, game nights, match days.
  • 3Run a workshop — teach what you know; learn the rest together.
  • 4Propose a new hub — bring your interest and a few friends.
  • 5Volunteer or sponsor — time, skills, venues or funding for real activity.

What happens after you send this?

1. A human reads every proposal. 2. Genuine proposals get a reply, usually within a week. 3. Good fits become a call, a pilot session, or a plan — your call.

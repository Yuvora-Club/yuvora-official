Future Hubs

More interests. More possibilities.

Music, art, writing, entrepreneurship, science — whatever the community dreams up next. New hubs start here, proposed by members like you.

How a new hub is born

  • 1Bring an interest — anything with at least a few curious humans behind it.
  • 2Run a pilot session — one pop-up to test the energy, supported by us.
  • 3Gather your crew — pilots that click earn a permanent home and colour.
  • 4Cross-pollinate — the best ideas mix hubs: beats × code, art × sport.

Future Hubs is a launchpad, not a waiting room. Pilot sessions and pop-ups run here all year.

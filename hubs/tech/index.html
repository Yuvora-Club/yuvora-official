Tech Hub

Build. Code. Create.

For people who love making things with technology — coding, robotics, design, and everything in between. Bring curiosity; leave with something you built.

What happens here

  • ✳Coding sessions & build nights — ship real things, from first webpages to full projects.
  • ✳Robotics & electronics tinkering — wires, sensors and gloriously blinking lights.
  • ✳Design & creative tools — because how it looks is part of how it works.
  • ✳Show-and-tell demos — finished (and half-finished) work gets its applause.

No experience needed. No expensive gear needed. Just show up — mentors and spare laptops do the rest.

# Yuvora — Creative Direction: "The Open Studio"

## Concept
Yuvora is a shared workshop. Different benches (hubs) sit in one bright
room, and the same people move between them. "Find your people" is the
hubs; "Make your thing" is the projects. The site feels warm, capable
and slightly handmade — a well-run studio with good light — never a tech
product, gaming arena, school portal, or SaaS dashboard.

## Why this direction
- **Welcoming first.** A first-time visitor understands Yuvora in five
  seconds: headline, one honest line, one real action. Curiosity comes
  from clarity plus one human sketch, not spectacle.
- **One identity across interests.** Bench labels, index rows and one
  ink/paper/sun system make tech, sport and gaming feel like variations
  in one room — so future hubs slot in without a redesign.
- **Content leads.** No photos exist in the repo, so imagery is honest:
  one labelled sketch and abstract hub glyphs, all marked PLACEHOLDER,
  never posed as real members or events. Real projects/events take over
  the same slots by adding data, not layouts.
- **Restrained craft.** Moderate type (h1 ≤ 4rem), hairlines, sharp
  2px edges, one hand-drawn underline used once, zero looping motion.
  AA contrast verified computationally for every pairing.

## Preserved
Vite MPA + vanilla stack, all 12 routes, `config.js`/`data.js` content
model, all real copy, demo-mode form honesty, nav/footer/a11y/SEO
patterns, Bricolage + Inter, brand SVGs.

## Changed
Dark cinematic system replaced with the bright studio system; homepage
rescoped to hero → hub index → making/happening previews → join steps;
empty states rebuilt as labelled "open bench" slots; hub-colored small
text moved to AA-safe darker variants.

## Reference insights (only what actually rendered)
- **clubs.hackclub.com (inspected):** learn plain visitor-addressed copy,
  one repeated join action, and concrete named programs; reject the
  maximalist busy hero — decoration competes with content — and its
  single-interest framing and photo dependence.
- **hackclub.com:** metadata only, no usable content. **HCB:** a sign-in
  wall, not the marketing site. **E-Summit:** returned nothing usable.
  **Wix templates:** require JS rendering unavailable here. None of these
  were copied; the identity is Yuvora's own.
